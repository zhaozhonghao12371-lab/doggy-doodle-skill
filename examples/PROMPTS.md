# Middle-version regression cards

All tests lock one Series Blueprint: square card, source-informed broad pose, bold repaired outer contour, simple round/dot eyes, graphic nose and chunky paws. Rotate one flat fibrous background for coat contrast. In text-rich mode, surround the pet with one headline plus 2–4 short phrases totaling 12–22 words; use light lettering on dark backgrounds and dark lettering on light backgrounds. In no-text mode, use 4–8 pet-responsive marks. Apply only 15%–20% controlled messiness through local repairs, open joins, overshoots and color misregistration.

## A — White schnauzer-like dog

Keep only: white/cream coat, low hanging ears, square face tendency, broad beard cue. Discard exact gaze, facial coordinates, beard flow, paws and source pose.

Messiness target: repaired ear turn and forehead tuft, doubled beard corners, paw overshoots, sparse blue/gray construction flecks and one imperfect speech-bubble closure.

Background/accent target: cobalt, turquoise or coral ground; loose ear-side arcs plus broken beard waves and one heart bubble.

Text target: `GOOD DOGGY`, `ONLY YOU`, `MY BEST GIFT`; cream text on cobalt or charcoal text on a light ground.

## B — Apricot teddy / bichon

Keep only: apricot coat, buried hanging ears, round face/body tendency, high fluff. Discard curl flow, exact muzzle, expression and stance.

Background/accent target: cobalt, turquoise, mint or dusty lilac; uneven cloud loops plus one tiny star.

Text target: `HAPPY`, `SO FLUFFY`, `LITTLE HAPPINESS`; strong value contrast and no more than three cloud-loop marks.

## C — Corgi

Keep only: orange/cream blocks, upright ears, long/low tendency, one mildly leaning ear. Discard exact blaze, expression, pose and ear angles.

Background/accent target: high-contrast cool ground; upright-ear rays plus one long horizontal swash.

Text target: one large `HAPPY DOGGY` headline and 2–3 short side phrases.

## D — Ordinary mixed-breed dog

Keep only: cocoa coat, one upright and one folded ear, longer face tendency, cream chest patch. Discard exact mouth, nose angle, patch shape, tail bend and stance.

Background/accent target: light contrasting ground; rays on the upright-ear side, one loose curve on the folded-ear side and a tiny irregular dot cluster.

Text target: 3–5 uneven phrases filling both sides while leaving the mismatched ears unobscured.

For every case target 45%–60% resemblance and use the complete negative list in `references/FAILURE_CASES.md`.

## E — Running puppy paired output

Pose Card: preserve running direction, stretched fluffy torso, one lifted front paw and raised curled tail. Do not convert to sitting and do not trace exact stride anatomy.

Keep only: broad cream/tan/white coat blocks, small upright ears, compact fluffy build and one red scarf cue.

No-text target: 4–8 speed, bounce and tail-echo marks. Text-rich target: derive from the no-text pose master, preserve the same running silhouette and place short phrases in the open motion wedges.
