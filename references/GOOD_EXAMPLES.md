# GOOD EXAMPLES — Series first, hints second

Every example uses the same square card, bold repaired outline, simple round/dot eyes, graphic nose, chunky paws, rough fill and text-rich surrounding grammar, while the broad pose follows the source photo.

The shared finish is deliberately a little messy: several local doubled strokes, a few open joins or overshoots, color that occasionally misses the contour and uneven accents. The disorder stays local and never hides the face or silhouette.

## A. White schnauzer-like dog

Keep only: white/cream coat; low hanging ears; slightly square face; one beard cue.

Discard: exact worried gaze, true eye spacing, broad-nose relationship, real beard flow, exact front paws and source stance.

Result: unmistakably part of the series; it hints at a white schnauzer-like pet without reconstructing this dog's face.

Messiness: a repaired ear turn, doubled beard corner, two paw overshoots, small blue/gray stray marks and cream color that slips across one beard edge.

Decoration: use cobalt, turquoise or coral for contrast; echo the hanging ears with two loose side arcs and the beard with 2–3 broken waves, then add one small heart bubble.

Lettering: a large `GOOD DOGGY` headline, `ONLY YOU` on one side and `MY BEST GIFT` on the other. Use cream letters on cobalt; use charcoal letters on mint or warm cream.

## B. Apricot teddy / bichon

Keep only: apricot coat; ears partly buried in fluff; round face/body tendency; high fluff level.

Discard: exact ear-cheek merge, real muzzle offset, specific resting pose and curl flow.

Result: the series face and body grammar remain stable, while warm color and round buried-ear silhouette distinguish it.

Decoration: use cobalt, turquoise, mint or dusty lilac; repeat the round fluff as 3–4 uneven cloud loops and add one tiny star or heart.

Lettering: `HAPPY` above, `SO FLUFFY` and `LITTLE HAPPINESS` down the sides; reduce cloud loops to 2–3 when text is dense.

## C. Corgi

Keep only: orange-white major color blocks; upright ears; long/low body tendency; one mildly uneven ear.

Discard: exact blaze path, skeptical gaze, source stance and precise ear angles.

Result: more elongated and alert than the fluffy dogs, but still clearly drawn by the same series system.

Decoration: use upright-ear rays plus one elongated horizontal swash that echoes the long body; keep the remaining background quiet.

Lettering: `HAPPY DOGGY` above and two short side phrases; use pale cream text on a dark blue background or deep navy text on a light background.

## D. Ordinary mixed-breed dog

Keep only: cocoa coat; one upright and one folded ear; slightly longer face; one simple cream chest patch.

Discard: exact worried mouth, nose angle, comma-patch shape, tail bend and stance.

Result: it avoids invented breed shorthand while retaining enough asymmetry and color placement to suggest a different source.

Decoration: choose a contrasting light ground; place short rays beside the upright ear and one loose curve beside the folded ear, with a tiny dot cluster echoing the chest patch.

Lettering: stagger 3–5 short phrases asymmetrically so the copy fills both sides without hiding the different ear shapes.

## E. Running puppy

Pose Card: running or quick-leaping to one side; preserve body direction, one lifted front paw and raised curved tail. Simplify the torso into one long fluffy mass and the legs into chunky gesture marks.

Keep only: broad coat blocks; ear family; compact/long tendency; one accessory or marking cue.

Discard: exact joint angles, stride mechanics, paw anatomy, photographic perspective and background.

No-text version: use speed curves, bounce ticks and a tail-echo spiral. Text-rich version: derive from the same pose master and place phrases in the open wedges around the moving silhouette.

## Useful prompt fragments

- `stylized pet doodle with light likeness`
- `decorative pet illustration with selective pet traits`
- `series consistency before resemblance`
- `same visual system, different pet hints`
- `45%–60% resemblance`
- `graphic pet character, not portrait`
- `simplified fluffy pet form`
- `keep 2–4 hints and discard exact facial evidence`
- `controlled messiness, 15%–20% more disorder`
- `localized repairs, open joins and color misregistration`

## Dangerous shortcuts

- `exact pet likeness`, `observational portrait`, `weirdly accurate` push too portrait-like;
- `simple cute dog`, `kid drawing`, `puppy icon`, `breed mascot` push too generic or childish;
- `copy the reference style exactly` risks imitation instead of transferable series mechanics.
